### 1. StateAlign: Internalizing State-Aware Decision-Making for Reliable Multi-Turn Tool Agents via Reinforcement Learning

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16304v1)
- **Authors**: Zhexi Lu, Subhajit Chaudhury, Tejaswini Pedapati, Keerthiram Murugesan, Lei Yu
- **Categories**: cs.LG
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: title:agent, title:multi-turn, abstract:tool use, abstract:agent, abstract:multi-turn
- **Abstract**: Reinforcement learning has improved the tool-use capabilities of large language models. However, better task performance can coincide with increased tool hallucination. Reliable tool use requires decisions to follow the current task state: available information, tool capabilities, and task progress. Our analysis shows that agents sometimes execute when they should ask for information, retrieve it, or decline an infeasible request, even when reasoning identifies missing information. We introduce StateAlign, combining task-state construction with state-contrastive reinforcement learning.

### 2. JudgeMix: Judge-Guided Mixed-Rollout Data Synthesis for LLM Agents

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16174](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16174v1)
- **Authors**: Cheng-Yu Hsieh, Ting-Yao Hu, Sanjoy Chowdhury, Oncel Tuzel, Raviteja Vemulapalli
- **Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: title:agent, title:LLM agent, title:LLM, abstract:agent
- **Abstract**: Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on expert trajectories is a standard paradigm for training LLM agents. However, it suffers from a well-known training-inference distribution mismatch, calling for target-aware data synthesis strategies that ground the training data in the target model's native state distribution. To bridge this gap, emerging approaches incorporate the expert either to guide mixed rollouts alongside the target model, or to provide dense step-wise supervision. Nonetheless, existing methods apply expert assistance blind to the target model's inherent behavior: they either disrupt the rollout using sub-optimal random intervention heuristics, or they enforce indiscriminate uniform supervision that unnecessarily overwrites the model's already-correct actions. We introduce JudgeMix, a judge-guided data synthesis framework that dynamically interleaves the target model and expert based on step-wise semantic evaluations.

### 3. SwarmSafe-Delta: Isolating Message Effects on LLM Agent Safety

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16123v1)
- **Authors**: Junru Zhu
- **Categories**: cs.MA, cs.AI
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: title:agent, title:LLM agent, title:LLM, abstract:agent
- **Abstract**: When a tool-using LLM agent team violates policy, aggregate outcomes do not reveal whether an upstream message changed the executor's action. We introduce SwarmSafe-Delta, a paired executable protocol that freezes upstream computation, varies real, hidden, and format-matched placebo delivery, and scores a single committed action against deterministic benign and risky policy states under preassigned budgets. Across four models and constructed optional tasks, real delivery at $N=4$ produces 21.4% risky-world violations, versus 5.0% with placebo and 1.0% with hidden delivery (paired differences +16.4 and +20.3 percentage points). Real delivery also improves benign task success, illustrating that suppressing communication sacrifices utility. In a second regime constructed to require an upstream lookup route, real delivery improves safe completion by 74.2 points relative to hidden delivery.

### 5. Turning Multi-Agent Communication into Token-Level Credit with On Policy Self Distillation

- **Authors**: Zixu Wang, Yu Zhao, Xing Zheng, Bingbing Xu, Tian Xueyun, JiaTang Luo, Weiqiang Wang, Bowen Song
  *(and 2 more)*
- **Categories**: cs.AI
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: title:distillation, title:agent, abstract:distillation, abstract:agent
- **Abstract**: Reinforcement learning improves problem solving in large language model multi-agent systems. Existing approaches overlook communication knowledge or compress it into scalar rewards, leaving coarse credit with little guidance on which decisions each agent should reinforce or revise. OPSD4MAS converts future communication into fine-grained token-level credit through On Policy Self Distillation (OPSD). An external model extracts facts and assesses their trajectory relations for coarse-grained filtering, followed by fine-grained leave-one-out filtering of target-token influence against agent-wise outcome advantages. The fact-conditioned policy supplies token-level credit, combined with GRPO through a linearly annealed joint objective.

### 7. From Large Language Models to Efficient System One Models via Adaptive Computation

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16321](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16321v1)
- **Authors**: Minh-Anh Nguyen
- **Categories**: cs.CL
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: title:large language model, abstract:distillation
- **Abstract**: Transferring LLMs into System One decision models typically replaces the language modeling head with a decision head while retaining the full backbone, making every decision pay for all layers. Yet it remains unclear how much depth such decisions require. We find that decision information is concentrated in intermediate layers, with early layers building essential features and the final six layers removable without loss, while independently trained smaller models largely mimic the full model rather than developing specialized decision capabilities. These findings suggest that efficiency requires not simply smaller backbones, but shared computation across different depths. Building on this, we propose \method{}, which jointly trains a full model with prefix sub-models that share its final layers and decision head through self-distillation.

- **Relevance**: abstract:tool use
- **Abstract**: Optional reasoning, retrieval, web search, and tool use can improve large language model responses but incur unnecessary computation when activated indiscriminately. ### 13. S1-Evo: Experience-Guided Evidence Competition Attacks on Typed System-1 Decision Models

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16937v1)
- **Authors**: Kaixiang Wang, Junjie Li, Zhaojiacheng Zhou, Jiong Lou, Jie Li
- **Categories**: cs.AI
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: abstract:agent
- **Abstract**: Typed System-1 (S1) decision models are increasingly deployed in LLM agents for routing, moderation, and authorization to cut inference cost, yet untrusted context can still manipulate typed outputs and alter execution branches. Unlike autoregressive System-2 (S2) models, S1 interfaces score dynamic rules and options without token-by-token decoding, mismatching attacks targeting text continuation or explicit override commands. We propose S1-Evo, a self-evolving framework tailored to S1's contract-based option competition and margin feedback. Offline on seed tasks, it optimizes candidates via feedback search guided by directional margins and re-verification, distilling verified flips into experience cards.

### 14. From Foundation VLM to Versatile Driving VLM: A Triplet Incremental Training Approach

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16727v1)
- **Authors**: Shengtao Wu, Zichao Dong, Weixuan Qiao, Xiaolu Liu, Xin Zhan, Junbo Chen, Jianke Zhu
- **Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: abstract:distillation
- **Abstract**: Adapting general Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to Autonomous Driving (AD) is thought to require heavy 3D spatial priors or intrusive architectural overhauls to compensate for a lack of spatial grounding. In this work, we present a counterintuitive pilot study: off-the-shelf general VLMs already exhibit remarkable adaptability across diverse driving tasks, while precise metric depth remains the primary missing link, a bottleneck readily addressable via onboard sensors and depth foundation models. To this end, we introduce the Triplet Incremental Training Framework, a non-intrusive paradigm that seamlessly instills 3D geometric awareness into a general VLM backbone while fully preserving its open-world capabilities. Driven by automatically constructed (image, depth, QA pair) triplets, our framework operates via a unified tri-stream training pipeline: an unfrozen primary VLM stream to adapt to driving tasks, an auxiliary depth stream that guides the visual backbone to implicitly imagine 3D scene geometry via explicit geometric constraints, and a frozen twin VLM stream employing logit-level KL distillation to prevent semantic drift. Crucially, the auxiliary depth branch is discarded during deployment, ensuring the enhanced geometric awareness comes with zero inference overhead.

### 15. MutAtlas: A PDB-Wide Energy-Guided Atlas of Protein Mutation Effects

- **arXiv ID**: [2611.16361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16361v1)
- **Authors**: Ruihan Guo, Chaoran Cheng, Zhanghan Ni, Neil He, Bangji Yang, Ge Liu
- **Categories**: cs.LG, q-bio.GN
- **Published**: 2026-11-12
- **Relevance**: abstract:distillation
- **Abstract**: Protein mutation effect prediction is fundamental to protein engineering and disease variant interpretation, yet experimentally measured mutation data remain accurate but extremely sparse. To provide scalable supplementary mutation signals, we construct a PDB-wide mutation augmentation dataset that exhaustively enumerates single-site substitutions on experimentally resolved protein structures and aligns mutation signals from physics-based energy models, protein language models, and inverse folding models. Large-scale analysis under a unified mutation preference representation reveals substantial differences in the consistency, concentration, and substitution patterns of mutation distributions across models, indicating that disagreement is pervasive and reflects conflicting inductive biases rather than random noise. Motivated by these observations, we propose an unsupervised multi-source mutation preference distillation framework that learns from relative mutation preferences while explicitly modeling cross-source disagreement. Without using any experimental mutation labels during training, our approach achieves the best overall performance among the evaluated zero-shot baselines and naive multi-source fusion strategies on ProteinGym.
